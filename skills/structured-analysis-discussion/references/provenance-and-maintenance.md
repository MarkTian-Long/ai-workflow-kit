## Validation and lifecycle

Status remains `validating`: the workflow may be used within its stated trigger while its decision value, rework, costs and burden are observed. This label is maintained by the method owner; it is not a Codex installation or enablement switch. Invocation policy is separately declared in agents/openai.yaml.

Private task receipts and machine-specific maintenance records are intentionally excluded. Their existence does not transfer a performance guarantee to another environment. The recipient should observe naturally eligible tasks in its existing records, distinguishing recognition, correct execution, concrete method gain and downstream result. Controlled synthetic applications are evidence for their tested scope, not independent real use, implicit discovery, long-term benefit or cost reduction. Do not generate tasks merely to increase counts.

The six-step workflow derives necessary capabilities, relationships and conditions before choosing solutions. It applies only to the unresolved analysis in mixed tasks and preserves fixed user choices. When maintaining reusable guidance, test the transferable relation against another task type or a counterexample within its stated scope.

## Upgrade, narrowing and retirement

Review upstream only when a real task exposes a gap, a frozen source materially changes, a license/security boundary changes, or repeated eligible use shows no net benefit. Stars alone are not an adoption gate.

For an upgrade, freeze the new source; inspect the relevant diff, license, dependencies, network behavior, telemetry and data flow; compare on eligible frozen tasks; retain previous bytes for rollback until validation closes. Changes to publication authority, installation, permissions or formal promotion still require their applicable authorization.

Narrow the trigger or propose disabling implicit invocation when perspectives repeat one another, decision-relevant evidence is not added, user correction/rework persists, or time, context, coordination, privacy or maintenance cost grows without useful gain. Retire when no active consumer remains or another maintained mechanism fully covers the purpose; preserve the reason in the applicable existing record. Do not silently change invocation policy or claim promotion from a count of uses.

Do not install the source frameworks, upload internal material, change model defaults, or promote multi-agent execution merely because this Skill references those projects.
